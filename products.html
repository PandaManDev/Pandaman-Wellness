General reference

Product category guide.

A neutral glossary of terms that may appear in product descriptions and packaging. This page is informational only — it is not a product menu or offer to sell.

Category glossary

Common names, simply explained.

Names and terminology can vary by region, manufacturer, and applicable regulations.

✿

Flower

Refers to the dried flower portion of the cannabis plant. Product labels and composition can vary.

⌁

Pre-rolls

A term commonly used for pre-rolled products containing dried plant material.

◉

Cartridges

Small containers designed for use with compatible vaporizer hardware. Contents and specifications vary.

✧

Concentrates

A broad category for products made by concentrating certain compounds from cannabis material.

⌖

Disposable vaporizers

Self-contained electronic devices that combine a power source and a prefilled cartridge or chamber.

❋

Tinctures

Liquid preparations typically supplied in a bottle with a dropper. Always read the product label carefully.

✳

Edibles

Food or beverage products that contain cannabis-derived ingredients. Labels may list serving and package amounts separately.

◌

Water pipes

Reusable devices that pass smoke through water. Commonly called bongs.

♢

Hand pipes

Small handheld smoking devices, often made from glass or other materials.

✦

Accessories

A general term for non-product items and related equipment. Materials and intended uses differ.

Important information

Read labels. Know local rules.

Cannabis products can cause impairment and may carry health risks. Keep any such products secured away from children and pets, do not drive while impaired, and follow all applicable age, possession, and local laws. For health questions, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
